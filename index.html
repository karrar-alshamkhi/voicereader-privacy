Privacy Policy for VoiceReader Mobile

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Alshamkhi Tech Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VoiceReader Mobile application (Package Name: com.alshamkhitech.voicereader). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data when using our application.

1. Information Collection and Use

VoiceReader Mobile is designed to convert text to speech for personal productivity. We value your privacy and aim to collect as little data as possible:

  • Text Data: Any text entered or imported into the app for text-to-speech processing is processed locally on your device or via native system TTS engines. We do not store, record, or transmit your text to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The app does not contain third-party advertisements or invasive tracking services. We do not sell or monetize your personal data.

3. Children's Privacy

VoiceReader Mobile does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard practices to ensure your information remains secure on your local device.
